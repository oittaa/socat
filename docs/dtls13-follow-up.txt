DTLS 1.3 remaining work

Status is in docs/dtls13.md; RFCs and code map in docs/dtls13-standards.md.
Follow AGENTS.md for platforms, compatibility and validation.

Merge

- Independent protocol/security review of internal/dtls13 and
  internal/xio/dtlsopen, including RFC 9846 section 1.2. Observable
  regression per defect. Do not relax certificate checks or add
  DTLS 1.2/browser workarounds.
- Fill local CID coverage gaps against RFC 9147 section 9: request/response,
  empty/bounded replies, spare consumption, rotation/retirement, loss/reorder,
  concurrent KeyUpdate, malformed messages and resource bounds. Include
  RFC-derived wire cases independent of our encoder. RFC-based review is
  required even though spare-CID interoperability is not a merge blocker.

Do not undo

- Listener.receive has one production caller; do not "fix" a duplicate
  admission race that is not concurrent.
- RFC 9147 section 5.11: after a verified Finished, abandon the previous
  same-address association. No CID exemption.
- Keep proactive CID requests. Delaying until movement cannot get spares
  on the old path. Disabling migration is a separate option.
- Immediate CID rotation must update pending path probes and leave
  unrelated spare requests alone.

Not merge blockers

- Spare-CID issuance/replenishment interoperability: pin an exact revision
  when a reference peer supports it; test both roles, repeated replenishment,
  loss/reorder and KeyUpdate. Do not build another stack or weaken our
  protocol for incomplete peers. See dtls13.md for current peer limits.
- Automatic CID pool renewal after consumed identifiers; see dtls13.md.
- Independent PQ tests at MTU 1200/256/512 with loss/reorder and mutual
  ML-DSA. Do not raise production MTU or drop PQ defaults as a "fix."
- Lab-only BoringSSL packet-BIO adapter; keep out of ordinary make check.
- Recheck official OpenSSL/socat releases when 4.1 is usable; do not patch
  the parity baseline or replace system OpenSSL to obtain a test peer.
- On Go upgrades, investigate TestGoTLS13AlgorithmDefaults drift: add DTLS
  wire mappings, retain protocol restrictions and test interoperability.
  Recheck the TLS ML-DSA mapping when its RFC is published.

Out of scope unless separately specified

- PSK, resumption, 0-RTT, post-handshake client authentication, extra AEADs,
  WebRTC/ICE/SRTP.
